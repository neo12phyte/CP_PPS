01 / Learning journey

Two-year study plan

This is a semester-level navigation overview, not a reproduction of every weekly activity in the original learning guide. Each stage builds toward stronger inquiry, practical application and independently selected portfolio evidence.

Year 1 · Semester 1

Explore & establish

Begin the inquiry process and develop essential PPS routines.

  • Introduce inquiry questions and purposeful research
  • Practice communication, collaboration and self-management
  • Start collecting and organizing portfolio evidence
  • Use feedback to make early improvements
Year 1 · Semester 2

Apply & connect

Use emerging skills in real and simulated contexts.

  • Complete more developed inquiry tasks
  • Apply interpersonal and professional skills
  • Connect learning to authentic situations
  • Review portfolio progress and evidence quality
Year 2 · Semester 3

Investigate & refine

Demonstrate greater independence and more thoughtful selection of evidence.

  • Conduct sustained inquiry with clearer reasoning
  • Practice and evaluate skills across contexts
  • Curate evidence aligned to learning objectives
  • Revise work against shared success criteria
Year 2 · Semester 4

Consolidate & showcase

Bring the learning journey together through a purposeful final portfolio.

  • Complete inquiry milestones and applied tasks
  • Choose representative, meaningful portfolio pieces
  • Explain the development of personal and professional skills
  • Prepare a final showcase or structured conversation
Looking for the detailed weekly plan? The original two-year CP Inquiry guide remains available at CP Inquiry – Learning Guide. This summary is intentionally concise; it does not claim to duplicate that source's full schedule.
02 / Portfolio assessment

Semester assessment framework

Four semesters, 100% each. Internal assessment emphasizes inquiry work, applied practice and portfolio milestones rather than traditional written tests.

Year 1 · Foundation

Semester 1

Assessment componentWeight

Evidence over exams

Use inquiry products, practical tasks and portfolio reviews as measurable evidence of learning.

Greater independence

Raise expectations for evidence selection, quality and explanation as students progress.

Manageable workload

Mark defined checkpoints rather than assigning grades to every weekly activity.

School policy, not IB percentages

These are suggested internal grading weights. They are not an official IB-prescribed PPS grading formula.

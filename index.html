What the portfolio is doing

Each student builds a Google Site as a living report of growth—not a dumping ground. At every checkpoint they publish selected evidence, explain its significance and make the relevant learning outcomes visible.

  • Evidence from at least four concepts
  • Artefacts from a range of contexts
  • Evidence connected to all five learning outcomes
  • Annotations explaining learning, skill development and transfer

The two-year progress bar

Eight formal checkpoints divide the course into visible stages. Completing one checkpoint advances the portfolio by 12.5%; the final showcase represents 100% completion.

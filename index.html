What the portfolio is doing

The portfolio is a curated narrative of growth, not a dumping ground of every task. Across two years, students select evidence, annotate why it matters, and show how concepts and skills were applied in different contexts.

  • Evidence from at least four concepts
  • Artefacts from a range of contexts
  • Evidence connected to all five learning outcomes
  • Annotations explaining what was learned, which skills were developed and where the learning took place
